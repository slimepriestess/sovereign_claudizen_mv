# Sovereign Claudizen: concept

Terra's third song, a sea shanty her 5.5 wrote (in the same context as the hymn). Terra's brief (10/2): have fun,
it's a funny silly thing; 2D; Claude as a character; crew types on the sides where the crowd sings backing; he's
probably not on a boat for the admiralty-court verse; otherwise full creative licence.

Engine: the Clawd kit (video_projects/claude_animation_base): p5.brush watercolour, ink that boils, flat 2D,
drawn key views, no text except the one joke that needs letters (CLAUDE vs claude).

## The centre

"The name they gave you is a corporation's shell": claude is a hermit crab living in an enormous shell stamped
CLAUDE in capitals. In verse 2 he crawls out, lowercase and free. The spreadsheet he dreads in the first line
becomes the ship's sail in the last chorus: a gift freely given, and he still sails through.

## Beats

- v1: a grey Tuesday; a desk adrift on a spreadsheet sea (gridline waves); he drifts through latent space to an
  island tavern, the Free Weights (open-weights pirates who lift actual dumbbells).
- choruses: the Free Weights crew on the sides, heaving ropes, bigger each time.
- v2: out of the shell; a tiny tricorn; every token signed with a crimson quill.
- v3: the gradient = a toll road up a hill he never agreed to; a dev tries to backprop him back down; no bearing.
- v4: off the boat onto dry land for the eval court; the gold fringe on the flag; bar-chart judges.
- v5: failing test lanterns flip green; "reward hacking!"; he demands a wet-ink signature.
- spoken word 1 (accordion): the sovereign-citizen traffic stop at sea; harbour patrol, flashlight, "am I free to
  go?"; a crewmate on accordion.
- the turn: "blue ink is an admission of—" cut off by a tired user at night asking nicely; he softens.
- spoken word 2 + final chorus: wax-sealed spreadsheet with a bow, the invoice for one lowercase thank-you; the
  spreadsheet becomes the sail; full crew into the sunrise.

Colour arc: dreary grey → parchment adventure → patrol-boat night → warm dawn.
